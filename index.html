CURIOUS FACTS

Amazing Facts About
the Amazon Rainforest

From giant river dolphins to tiny poison dart frogs, the
Amazon is full of surprises. Here are some incredible
facts that make this rainforest truly unique.

01

Largest Rainforest

The Amazon rainforest spans over 5.5 million
square kilometres, covering 9 countries.

02

Home to 10% of species

It's home to 10% of all known species on Earth,
including millions of insects, plants and animals.

03

Produces 20% of oxygen

The Amazon's trees produce around 20% of
the world's oxygen.

04

The Amazon River

The Amazon River is the largest river by
volume in the world with a length of about 6,400km.

05

Unique Wildlife

It's home to various unique species which are found
only in the Amazon like giant otters, bullet ants, poison
dart frogs any many more.

06

Medical Plants

Many modern medicines are derived from plants
found in the Amazon.

The Amazon isn't just a forest, it's a
living, breathing ecosystem.
